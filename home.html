About PsyBuddy

PsyBuddy is an innovative assistive technology project developed within the UNICAMP ecosystem, focused on mental health and emotional support. The project addresses a critical gap in traditional psychological care: the isolation and lack of support patients face between weekly therapy sessions

Designed as an affective transitional object (a customizable plush toy), PsyBuddy conceals an advanced technological core without looking like a cold or stigmatizing medical device.

Key Features:

  • Adaptive AI (Multi-Persona)

    • Processes natural language to offer 24/7 empathetic support, adapting its tone from playful for children to cognitive-behavioral for adults

  • Haptic Co-Regulation

    • Features an internal electronic heart that simulates calming, rhythmic heartbeats. When squeezed during an anxiety or panic attack, it physically guides the user's nervous system back to a stable rhythm

  • Crisis Management (Panic Button)

    • A discreet safety mechanism that triggers immediate alerts to the patient's support network or medical team

  • Wireless Induction Charging

    • Eliminates exposed cables and ports, keeping the device entirely sealed and safe while making the plush casing 100% washable

Ethical Business Model:

PsyBuddy operates under an inclusive and ethical B2B2C framework. Patients are never charged subscription fees. Instead, the financial model relies on healthcare operators and psychology clinics that subscribe to a secure clinical dashboard to monitor patient progress data, while part of the corporate revenue subsidizes units for social projects and low-income clinics.
